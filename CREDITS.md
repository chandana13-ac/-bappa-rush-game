# 📜 Credits & Attributions

## 🐘 Bappa Rush: The Great Festival Challenge

### 🎨 Concept & Game Design
- **Inspiration**: Traditional Ganesh Chaturthi festival celebrations, community pandal volunteers, Maharashtrian dhol-tasha, rangoli art, and festive modak sweets.
- **Visual Aesthetic**: Low-poly warm nighttime courtyard lit with brass diyas, marigold floral garlands (torans), royal purple mandap draping, and golden fairy lights.

### 💻 Technology & Libraries
- **React & TypeScript**: UI component architecture and type safety.
- **Three.js**: 3D scene rendering, procedural geometries, materials, dynamic lights, and particle systems.
- **Vite**: Rapid frontend development and build tooling.
- **Tailwind CSS**: Warm festive typography, layout, and HUD styling.
- **Lucide Icons**: Clean, crisp iconography.
- **Web Audio API**: Real-time procedural audio synthesis for temple bells, dhol beats, chimes, and ambient drones.

### 🛡️ Open Source & Copyright Compliance
- **Zero Copyrighted Assets**: No proprietary images, music tracks, or downloaded 3D model meshes were used.
- **Self-Contained Audio**: Generated dynamically in-browser using mathematical oscillator waveforms.
- **Procedural Low-Poly Art**: Created exclusively from basic geometric primitives (cubes, cylinders, spheres, cones, rings, and bezier curves).
